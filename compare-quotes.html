💡 Tips for Comparing Quotes

  • ✅ Check what's included: Make sure both quotes cover the same scope. A cheaper quote might exclude important items.
  • ✅ Look at turnaround time: Faster isn't always better. Quality analysis takes time.
  • ✅ Ask about revisions: Can you get follow-up questions answered? What's the revision policy?
  • ✅ Verify credentials: Check reviews, look at sample reports, and verify their methodology.
  • ✅ Consider value, not just price: The cheapest isn't always the best. Factor in quality, support, and expertise.
