Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take?

Most websites are built within 48 hours of receiving your information. Complex properties with many room types may take 3–5 days.

Do I need to know how to code?

Not at all. You answer a few questions via WhatsApp — we handle the rest. If you can send a WhatsApp message, you can get a website.

What photos do I need to send?

Ideally: exterior, at least one room, bathroom, and any outdoor areas (pool, braai, garden). If you don't have photos yet, we can use quality stock images as placeholders.

What hosting should I use?

We recommend Cloudflare Pages — it's free, fast, and reliable. We guide you through the setup step by step. Alternatively, Netlify or Vercel both have free tiers.

Can I update the website myself?

Yes — we deliver the source files. If you know basic HTML/CSS you can update it, or we can make updates for a small fee.

What about Airbnb and Lekkeslaap listings?

Your website complements your existing listings — it doesn't replace them. Many guests will Google your guesthouse name after seeing it on Airbnb. A professional website builds trust and drives direct bookings.

Can you add a booking system?

For the MVP, we recommend using WhatsApp as your booking channel — it's familiar to South African guests and costs nothing. For more complex needs (payment processing, calendar sync), we can discuss a custom solution.
