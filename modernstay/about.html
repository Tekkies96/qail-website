About ModernStay

Home away from home — since day one

— ✦ —

ModernStay began with a simple belief: every guest deserves more than a place to sleep. They deserve to arrive and feel genuinely cared for. To open the door and sense that someone thought about every detail — from fresh flowers on the counter to the perfectly stocked spice rack.

Today, ModernStay curates South Africa's most beloved short-stay properties. Each one chosen not just for its beauty, but for its ability to make guests feel at home.

Johannes, Jannie, and Susan built ModernStay around one idea: the difference between a good stay and an unforgettable one lives in the details. It's the Ferrero Rocher on the pillow. The welcome pack for the kids. The message checking in just to make sure everything's perfect.

We don't manage properties.
We create homes.
